# Phase 8, item 36: the 20 hardest pieces of text, with suggested wording

Command: `node audit/scripts/readability.mjs` (results: `audit/results/readability.json`). It scored 3,463 sentences from 1,846 pieces of text shown to people: every rule, notice, and education line in `data/conditions.json`, every article line in `data/articles.json`, and sentence-length text in the screens' code.

- Median reading grade (Flesch-Kincaid): **9.1**.
- 930 sentences score above grade 12, and 371 run longer than 25 words.
- 637 sentences use an abbreviation that is not spelled out in the same text (ADA, AGA, FODMAP, MCAS, SGLT2, INR ...).
- The articles' "What the evidence says" sections are citation lists by design (260 sentences, median grade 13.3). They are ranked separately, with one representative below.

The 20 below were picked from the top of the ranking and weighted toward what the lite build shows every day. A reading score alone misses jargon in short lines, so items 1 to 3 are short lines chosen by hand.

**Report only.** Nothing was changed. Items marked *clinical* are rule text: keep the clinical meaning, and re-read the cited source before changing the wording.
